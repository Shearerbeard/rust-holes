# Process feedback

Friction with this playbook, a rule that fought the work, a template that
did not instantiate cleanly, a limit the Known limits section does not
name, is signal for the practice, not just for your repo. Record it as it
happens in your repo's own friction log. Where your setup includes a
boardkit checkout (this playbook composes with boardkit but does not
require it), route the practice-relevant items to that checkout's
`FEEDBACK.md` inbox (`${BOARDKIT_HOME:-../boardkit}/FEEDBACK.md`), which
states the entry format; one inbox serves the whole kit family, this repo
included, and a maintainer session drains it. Without one, keep the
finding in your own friction log and raise it with the maintainer
directly. Either way, never edit this repo's playbook or templates from a
consumer repo.
